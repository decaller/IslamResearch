# Taxonomy of Munakahat - (تصنيف المناكحات)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Al-Munakahat (المناكحات), the branch of Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) dealing with marriage, family dynamics, separation, child custody, and the distribution of inheritance.

## 1. كتاب النكاح (Kitab al-Nikah - The Book of Marriage)
The rulings governing the formation of a valid marital contract, mutual rights, and prerequisites.

### 1.1 مقدمات وأحكام الخطبة (Muqaddimat wa Ahkam al-Khitbah - Rules of Engagement)
- **Bab: أحكام النظر والخطبة (Ahkam al-Nazar wa al-Khitbah - Looking at the Prospective Spouse and Proposal)**
- **Bab: المحرمات من النساء (Al-Muharramat min al-Nisa' - Women Forbidden for Marriage)**

### 1.2 أركان وشروط العقد (Arkan wa Shurut al-'Aqd - Pillars and Conditions of the Contract)
- **Bab: الولي والشهود (Al-Wali wa al-Shuhud - The Legal Guardian and Witnesses)**
- **Bab: الصيغة: الإيجاب والقبول (Al-Sighah - The Form: Offer and Acceptance)**
- **Bab: الكفاءة في النكاح (Al-Kafa'ah fi al-Nikah - Compatibility and Suitability)**

### 1.3 الصداق والحقوق (Al-Sadaq wa al-Huquq - Dowry and Mutual Rights)
- **Bab: أحكام المهر أو الصداق (Ahkam al-Mahr / Al-Sadaq - Rulings of the Dowry)**
- **Bab: حقوق الزوجين (Huquq al-Zawjayn - Mutual Rights and Obligations)**
- **Bab: القسم والنشوز (Al-Qasm wa al-Nushuz - Time-Sharing and Marital Discord)**

## 2. كتاب الفراق (Kitab al-Firaq - The Book of Separation)
Jurisprudence surrounding the dissolution of marriage.

### 2.1 الطلاق (Al-Talaq - Divorce)
- **Bab: الطلاق السني والبدعي (Al-Talaq al-Sunni wa al-Bid'i - Sunnah vs. Innovated Methods)**
- **Bab: صريح الطلاق وكنايته (Sarih al-Talaq wa Kinayatuh - Explicit vs. Implicit Expressions)**
- **Bab: الرجعة (Al-Raj'ah - Revoking Divorce and Resuming Marriage)**

### 2.2 الخلع والفسخ (Al-Khul' wa al-Faskh - Divorce by Wife and Annulment)
- **Bab: أحكام الخلع (Ahkam al-Khul' - Rulings of Wife-Initiated Divorce)**
- **Bab: الفسخ بسبب العيوب (Al-Faskh bi-sabab al-'Uyun - Annulment due to Defects)**

### 2.3 الأيمان الزوجية (Al-Ayman al-Zawjiyyah - Marital Oaths)
- **Bab: الإيلاء (Al-Ila' - Oath of Sexual Abstinence)**
- **Bab: الظهار (Al-Zihar - Equating Wife to Prohibited Relative)**
- **Bab: اللعان (Al-Li'an - Oath of Condemnation or Paternity Denial)**

## 3. كتاب العدة والنفقات (Kitab al-'Iddah wa al-Nafaqat - Waiting Periods and Maintenance)
Post-separation rulings and financial responsibilities.

### 3.1 أحكام العدة (Ahkam al-'Iddah - Rulings of the Waiting Period)
- **Bab: عدة المطلقة ('Iddat al-Mutallaqah - Waiting Period of the Divorced Woman)**
- **Bab: عدة المتوفى عنها زوجها ('Iddat al-Mutawaffa - Waiting Period of the Widow)**
- **Bab: الاستبراء (Al-Istibra' - Verification of an Empty Womb)**

### 3.2 النفقات (Al-Nafaqat - Financial Maintenance)
- **Bab: نفقة الزوجة والمطلقة (Nafaqat al-Zawjah wa al-Mutallaqah - Maintenance of Wife/Divorced Woman)**
- **Bab: نفقة الأقارب والأولاد (Nafaqat al-Aqarib wa al-Awlad - Maintenance of Children and Relatives)**

## 4. كتاب الحضانة والرضاع (Kitab al-Hadanah wa al-Rada' - Custody and Fosterage)
Rulings concerning infant nourishment and the rights involving child rearing.

### 4.1 الرضاعة (Al-Rada'ah - Breastfeeding and Fosterage)
- **Bab: ما يثبت به تحريم الرضاع (Ma Yuthbit bihi Tahrim al-Rada' - Establishing Fosterage Prohibition)**
- **Bab: أحكام الأمومة الرضاعية (Ahkam al-Umumah al-Rada'iyyah - Rulings of Foster Motherhood)**

### 4.2 الحضانة (Al-Hadanah - Child Custody)
- **Bab: من له حق الحضانة (Man Lahu Haqq al-Hadanah - Who holds Primary Right to Custody)**
- **Bab: شروط الحاضن وسقوط الحق (Shurut al-Hadin wa Suqut al-Haqq - Conditions and Loss of Right)**

## 5. كتاب الفرائض والوصايا (Kitab al-Fara'id wa al-Wasaya - Inheritance and Bequests)
Distribution of wealth left behind after a family member's passing.

### 5.1 المواريث (Al-Mawarith - Inheritance)
- **Bab: أسباب الميراث وموانعه (Asbab al-Mirath wa Mawani'uhu - Causes and Impediments)**
- **Bab: أصحاب الفروض (Ashab al-Furud - Primary Heirs with Fixed Shares)**
- **Bab: العصبة (Al-'Asabah - Agnatic / Residuary Heirs)**
- **Bab: الحجب (Al-Hajb - Exclusion and Reduction of Shares)**

### 5.2 الوصايا (Al-Wasaya - Bequests and Wills)
- **Bab: شروط الوصية (Shurut al-Wasiyyah - Conditions: The One-Third Limit)**
