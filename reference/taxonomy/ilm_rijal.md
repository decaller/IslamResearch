# Taxonomy of Ilm al-Rijal wa al-Jarh wa al-Ta'dil - (تصنيف علم الرجال والجرح والتعديل)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Ilm al-Rijal and the Science of Jarh wa al-Ta'dil (Narrator Evaluation). It focuses on the history, integrity, and memory of Hadith narrators to ensure the authenticity of the transmission chain (Isnad).

## 1. تاريخ الرواة (Tarikh al-Ruwat - History of Narrators)
The study of biographical details, timelines, and geographical contexts of narrators.

### 1.1 طبقات الرواة (Tabaqat al-Ruwat - Generations of Narrators)
- **Bab: معرفة الصحابة (Ma'rifat al-Sahabah - Identifying the Companions)**
- **Bab: معرفة التابعين وأتباعهم (Ma'rifat al-Tabi'in wa Atba'ihim - Identifying the Successors and their followers)**
- **Bab: مراتب الطبقات (Maratib al-Tabaqat - Distinguishing the ranks of generations)**

### 1.2 المواليد والوفيات (Mawalid wa Wafayat - Births and Deaths)
- **Bab: أهمية الوفيات في كشف التدليس والانقطاع (Ahammiyat al-Wafayat - Importance of death dates in exposing hidden gaps)**

### 1.3 رحلة الرواة (Rihlat al-Ruwat - Journeys of Narrators)
- **Bab: الرحلة في طلب الحديث (Al-Rihlah fi Talab al-Hadith - Traveling in search of Hadith)**
- **Bab: الأوطان والبلدان (Al-Awtan wa al-Buldan - Regions and cities of the narrators)**

## 2. علم الجرح والتعديل (Ilm al-Jarh wa al-Ta'dil - Science of Criticism and Praise)
Evaluating the integrity (Adalah) and memory (Dabt) of narrators.

### 2.1 أصول وقواعد الجرح والتعديل (Usul wa Qawa'id - Principles and Rules)
- **Bab: مشروعية الجرح والتعديل (Mashru'iyyat al-Jarh wa al-Ta'dil - Legitimacy of criticizing narrators)**
- **Bab: شروط المعدِّل والجارح (Shurut al-Mu'addil wa al-Jarih - Conditions of the critic and evaluator)**

### 2.2 مراتب التعديل (Maratib al-Ta'dil - Ranks of Praise/Validation)
- **Bab: الألفاظ الدالة على التوثيق المطلق (Al-Alfaz al-Dallah 'ala al-Tawthiq al-Mutlaq - Terms for absolute reliability: Thiqah Thiqah)**
- **Bab: الألفاظ الدالة على قبول الاعتبار (Al-Alfaz al-Dallah 'ala Qubul al-I'tibar - Terms for supporting reliability: Saduq)**

### 2.3 مراتب الجرح (Maratib al-Jarh - Ranks of Criticism/Invalidation)
- **Bab: الألفاظ الدالة على اللين (Al-Alfaz al-Dallah 'ala al-Layyin - Terms for mild weakness: Layyin)**
- **Bab: الألفاظ الدالة على الترك والوضع (Al-Alfaz al-Dallah 'ala al-Tark wa al-Wad' - Terms for abandonment or fabrication)**

### 2.4 تعارض الجرح والتعديل (Ta'arudh al-Jarh wa al-Ta'dil - Contradiction between Praise and Criticism)
- **Bab: الجرح المفسر مقدم على التعديل (Al-Jarh al-Mufassar Muqaddam - Explained criticism takes precedence over general praise)**

## 3. أسماء الرواة وأنسابهم (Asma' al-Ruwat wa Ansabuhum - Names and Lineages)
Identifying errors and distinguishing between identical or similar names.

### 3.1 المؤتلف والمختلف (Al-Mu'talif wa al-Mukhtalif - Similar Written, Different Pronounced)
- **Bab: ضبط الأسماء والأنساب (Dabt al-Asma' wa al-Ansab - Correcting pronunciation of names and lineages)**

### 3.2 المتفق والمفترق (Al-Muttafiq wa al-Muftariq - Identical Names, Different Persons)
- **Bab: تمايز الأشخاص بالصفات والتاريخ (Tamayuz al-Ashkhas - Distinguishing individuals by traits and history)**

### 3.3 الكنى والألقاب (Al-Kuna wa al-Alqab - Agnomens and Titles)
- **Bab: من اشتهر بكنيته دون اسمه (Man ishtahara bi-kunyatih - Those famous by agnomen rather than name)**
- **Bab: الألقاب الدالة على عيب (Al-Alqab al-Mudallah 'ala 'Ayb - Titles indicating physical or character defects)**

## 4. أحوال الرواة الخاصة (Ahwal al-Ruwat al-Khassah - Special Conditions of Narrators)
Studying specific groups of narrators with unique conditions.

### 4.1 الضعفاء والمتروكون (Al-Du'afa' wa al-Matrukun - The Weak and Abandoned)
- **Bab: أسباب الضعف في الحفظ والعدالة (Asbab al-Da'f - Causes of weakness in memory and integrity)**
- **Bab: الوضاعون والكذابون (Al-Wadda'un wa al-Kadhdhabun - Fabricators and Liars)**

### 4.2 المختلطون (Al-Mukhtalitun - Those with Deteriorated Memory)
- **Bab: معرفة من اختلط في آخر عمره (Ma'rifat man ikhtalata - Identifying those whose memory failed in old age)**
- **Bab: حكم رواية المختلط قبل وبعد الاختلاط (Hukm riwayat al-Mukhtalit - Rulings on narrations before vs. after deterioration)**

### 4.3 المدلسون (Al-Mudallisun - The Concealers/Omitters)
- **Bab: مراتب المدلسين وطبقاتهم (Maratib al-Mudallisin - Ranks and levels of those who practice Tadlis)**
