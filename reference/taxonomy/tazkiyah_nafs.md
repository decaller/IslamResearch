# Taxonomy of Tazkiyah al-Nafs - (تصنيف تزكية النفس)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Tazkiyah al-Nafs (تزكية النفس), the Islamic spiritual science focusing on the inner state of the believer, the purification of the heart from destructive traits, and the cultivation of saving virtues.

## 1. مقدمات في التزكية (Muqaddimat fi al-Tazkiyah - Introductions to Purification)
Foundational concepts regarding spiritual anatomy and the soul.

### 1.1 حقيقة النفس وأقسامها (Haqiqat al-Nafs wa Aqsamiha - Reality and Types of Soul)
- **Bab: النفس الأمارة بالسوء (The Soul that Commands to Evil)**
- **Bab: النفس اللوامة (The Self-Reproaching Soul)**
- **Bab: النفس المطمئنة (The Tranquil/At-Peace Soul)**

### 1.2 القلب وأحواله (Al-Qalb wa Ahwaluhu - The Heart and its Conditions)
- **Bab: القلب السليم (The Sound and Pure Heart)**
- **Bab: القلب الميت (The Dead Heart)**
- **Bab: القلب المريض (The Sick Heart)**

## 2. أمراض القلوب أو المهلكات (Amrad al-Qulub / Al-Muhlikat - Diseases and Destructive Traits)
Spiritual ailments and vices that corrupt the soul.

### 2.1 أمراض الشهوات والهوى (Amrad al-Shahawat wa al-Hawa - Diseases of Base Desires)
- **Bab: حب الدنيا وطول الأمل (Love of the World and Prolonged Hope)**
- **Bab: اتباع الهوى وشهوة البطن والفرج (Following Caprice and Desires of Stomach/Private Parts)**
- **Bab: الغضب والحقد (Anger and Deep-Seated Malice)**

### 2.2 أمراض الشبهات والقلوب (Amrad al-Shubuhat wa al-Qulub - Diseases of Doubts and the Heart)
- **Bab: الرياء والسمعة (Ostentation and Seeking Reputation)**
- **Bab: الكبر والغرور (Arrogance and Delusion)**
- **Bab: العجب (Vanity and Self-Conceit)**
- **Bab: الحسد (Envy and Resentment)**

## 3. علاج النفس وتطهيرها ('Ilaj al-Nafs wa Tathiruha - Curing and Cleansing)
Methodologies and exercises to combat destructive traits.

### 3.1 رياضة النفس (Riyadat al-Nafs - Disciplining the Soul)
- **Bab: المشارطة والمراقبة (Stipulating Conditions and Vigilance)**
- **Bab: محاسبة النفس (Self-Accountability and Introspection)**
- **Bab: معاقبة النفس ومعاتبتها (Penalizing and Reprimanding the Soul)**

### 3.2 المجاهدة والتخلية (Al-Mujahadah wa al-Takhliyah - Striving and Emptying)
- **Bab: مجاهدة الشيطان والهوى (Striving against Satan and Base Desires)**
- **Bab: قصر الأمل وذكر الموت (Shortening Worldly Hopes and Remembering Death)**
- **Bab: حفظ الجوارح (Guarding the Limbs: tongue, eyes, and ears)**

## 4. مقامات اليقين أو المنجيات (Maqamat al-Yaqin / Al-Munjiyat - Stations and Saving Virtues)
Progressive spiritual stations leading to Ihsan (Excellence).

### 4.1 المقامات الأساسية (Al-Maqamat al-Asasiyyah - Foundational Stations)
- **Bab: التوبة والاستغفار (Repentance and Seeking Forgiveness)**
- **Bab: الصبر والشكر (Patience and Gratitude)**
- **Bab: الخوف والرجاء (Fear and Hope)**

### 4.2 المقامات العليا (Al-Maqamat al-'Ulya - Higher Stations)
- **Bab: الزهد والورع (Asceticism/Detachment and Scrupulousness)**
- **Bab: التوكل والتفويض (Absolute Reliance and Delegation)**
- **Bab: المحبة والشوق (Divine Love and Yearning for Allah)**
- **Bab: الرضا والتسليم (Contentment and Complete Submission)**

## 5. غذاء الروح (Ghitha' al-Ruh - Nourishment of the Spirit)
Righteous acts that fuel the soul and heart.

### 5.1 الأعمال القلبية والقولية (Al-A'mal al-Qalbiyyah wa al-Qawliyyah - Heart and Tongue Actions)
- **Bab: فضائل الذكر وأثره على القلب (Virtues of Remembrance and its Impact)**
- **Bab: تلاوة القرآن بتدبر (Reciting the Quran with Contemplation)**
- **Bab: الدعاء والافتقار إلى الله (Supplication and Manifesting Neediness)**

### 5.2 النوافل والخلوات (Al-Nawafil wa al-Khulawat - Voluntary Acts and Seclusion)
- **Bab: قيام الليل ومناجاة الله (Night Prayer and Intimate Converse)**
- **Bab: العزلة والخلوة الشرعية (Lawful Seclusion and Withdrawal)**
