# Taxonomy of Al-Tarajim wa al-Tabaqat - (تصنيف التراجم والطبقات)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Al-Tarajim wa al-Tabaqat (التراجم والطبقات), the Islamic historical science dedicated to cataloging the biographies, chronological generations, and achievements of the Prophet's Companions, scholars, narrators, and notable historical figures.

## 1. أصول ومناهج التصنيف (Usul wa Manahij al-Tasneef - Principles and Compilation Methodologies)
Foundational concepts and structural methods used to organize biographical encyclopedias.

### 1.1 تعريف العلم وأهميته (Ta'rif al-'Ilm wa Ahammiyatuha - Definition and Importance)
- **Bab: الفرق بين الترجمة والطبقة (The Difference between a Biography and a Generation)**
- **Bab: أهمية علم الرجال في حفظ الدين (The Importance of Biographical Science in Preserving the Religion)**

### 1.2 طرق ترتيب التراجم (Turuq Tartib al-Tarajim - Methods of Ordering Biographies)
- **Bab: الترتيب على حروف المعجم (Alphabetical Arrangement)**
- **Bab: الترتيب على الطبقات والوفيات (Arrangement by Generations and Death Dates)**
- **Bab: الترتيب على البلدان (Arrangement by Regions and Cities)**

## 2. طبقات الصحابة والتابعين (Tabaqat al-Sahabah wa al-Tabi'in - Generations of Companions and Successors)
Biographical study of the first two generations of Islam.

### 2.1 معرفة الصحابة (Ma'rifat al-Sahabah - Identifying the Companions)
- **Bab: شروط الصحبة وفضائلهم (Conditions of Companionship and their Virtues)**
- **Bab: الصحابة المكثرون من الرواية (The Prolific Narrators among the Companions)**
- **Bab: المبشرون بالجنة وأهل بدر (Those Promised Paradise and the Veterans of Badr)**

### 2.2 طبقات التابعين (Tabaqat al-Tabi'in - Generations of the Successors)
- **Bab: كبار التابعين وفقهائهم (The Senior Successors and their Jurists)**
- **Bab: المخضرمون (Those who lived during both Jahiliyyah and Islam but did not meet the Prophet)**

## 3. طبقات العلماء حسب الفنون (Tabaqat al-'Ulama' hasab al-Funun - Generations of Scholars by Disciplines)
Categorizing scholars by the specific academic science they mastered.

### 3.1 طبقات المحدثين (Tabaqat al-Muhaddithin - Generations of Hadith Scholars)
- **Bab: حفاظ الحديث ونقاده (The Memorizers and Critics of Hadith)**
- **Bab: رواة الكتب الستة (The Narrators of the Six Canonical Books)**

### 3.2 طبقات الفقهاء (Tabaqat al-Fuqaha' - Generations of Jurists)
- **Bab: طبقات الحنفية والمالكية (Generations of the Hanafi and Maliki Scholars)**
- **Bab: طبقات الشافعية والحنابلة (Generations of the Shafi'i and Hanbali Scholars)**

### 3.3 طبقات القراء والمفسرين (Tabaqat al-Qurra' wa al-Mufassirin - Generations of Reciters and Exegetes)
- **Bab: تراجم القراء العشرة ورواتهم (Biographies of the Ten Reciters and their Transmitters)**
- **Bab: طبقات المفسرين (Generations of Quranic Exegetes)**

### 3.4 طبقات النحاة والأدباء (Tabaqat al-Nuhat wa al-Udaba' - Generations of Grammarians and Men of Letters)
- **Bab: طبقات نحاة البصرة والكوفة (Generations of the Basran and Kufan Grammarians)**
- **Bab: تراجم الشعراء والأدباء (Biographies of Poets and Literary Figures)**

## 4. التراجم البلدانية (Al-Tarajim al-Buldaniyyah - Regional and Territorial Biographies)
Encyclopedias dedicated to scholars, rulers, and notable figures from specific regions.

### 4.1 تواريخ المدن والأقاليم (Tarawikh al-Mudun wa al-Aqalim - Histories of Cities and Regions)
- **Bab: تاريخ بغداد ودمشق (The Biographical Histories of Baghdad and Damascus)**
- **Bab: علماء الأندلس والمغرب (Scholars of Andalusia and the Islamic West)**
- **Bab: علماء مكة والمدينة (Scholars of Mecca and Medina)**
- **Bab: تراجم علماء مصر واليمن (Biographies of the Scholars of Egypt and Yemen)**

## 5. التراجم العامة وتراجم النساء (Al-Tarajim al-'Ammah wa Tarajim al-Nisa' - General and Women's Biographies)
Comprehensive encyclopedias and works dedicated specifically to the achievements of women.

### 5.1 الموسوعات التاريخية والوفيات (Al-Mawsaw'at al-Tarikhiyyah wa al-Wafayat - Historical Encyclopedias and Obituaries)
- **Bab: وفيات الأعيان (Obituaries of Eminent Men)**
- **Bab: التراجم القرنية (Centennial Biographies: cataloging figures by their death century)**
- **Bab: التراجم المعاصرة (Contemporary and Modern Biographies)**

### 5.2 تراجم النساء (Tarajim al-Nisa' - Biographies of Women)
- **Bab: أعلام النساء من الصحابيات (Eminent Women among the Companions)**
- **Bab: المحدثات والفقيهات (Female Hadith Scholars and Jurists throughout history)**
