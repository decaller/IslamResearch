# Taxonomy of Al-Nasikh wa al-Mansukh - (تصنيف الناسخ والمنسوخ)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of the discipline of Al-Nasikh wa al-Mansukh (The Abrogating and the Abrogated). It details the theoretical principles of abrogation, its classifications, the methods used to identify it, and the specific domains where it is applicable.

## 1. مقدمات وأصول النسخ (Muqaddimat wa Usul al-Naskh - Introductions and Principles of Abrogation)
The foundational definitions, conceptual boundaries, and historical evolution of the term "Naskh" among Islamic scholars.

### 1.1 حقيقة النسخ (Haqiqat al-Naskh - The Reality/Definition of Abrogation)
- **Bab: تعريف النسخ لغة واصطلاحاً (Ta'rif al-Naskh Lughatan wa Istilahan - Linguistic and Technical Definition of Abrogation)**
- **Bab: الفرق بين النسخ والبداء (Al-Farq bayna al-Naskh wa al-Bada' - The Difference between Abrogation and 'Bada')**
- **Bab: الفرق بين النسخ والتخصيص (Al-Farq bayna al-Naskh wa al-Takhsis - Distinguishing Abrogation from Specification/Restriction)**

### 1.2 تطور مصطلح النسخ (Tatawwur Mustalah al-Naskh - Evolution of the Term)
- **Bab: مفهوم النسخ عند المتقدمين (Mafhum al-Naskh 'inda al-Mutaqaddimin/Al-Salaf - The broader early definition)**
- **Bab: مفهوم النسخ عند المتأخرين والأصوليين (Mafhum al-Naskh 'inda al-Muta'akhkhirin/Al-Usuliyyin - The narrower later definition)**

### 1.3 شروط النسخ (Shurut al-Naskh - Conditions for Abrogation)
- **Bab: تعارض الأدلة (Ta'arudh al-Adillah - The presence of an irreconcilable contradiction between texts)**
- **Bab: تراخي الناسخ عن المنسوخ (Tarakhi al-Nasikh 'an al-Mansukh - The abrogating text must chronologically follow the abrogated text)**

## 2. أقسام النسخ في القرآن (Aqsam al-Naskh fi al-Quran - Categories of Abrogation in the Quran)
The classification of abrogation based on what is being removed: the spoken text, the legal ruling, or both.

### 2.1 نسخ الحكم والتلاوة (Naskh al-Hukm wa al-Tilawah - Abrogation of both Ruling and Text)
- **Bab: الآيات التي نسخت لفظاً وحكماً (Al-Ayat allati nusikhat lafzan wa hukman - Examples of verses completely removed from the Mushaf and practice)**

### 2.2 نسخ التلاوة دون الحكم (Naskh al-Tilawah dun al-Hukm - Abrogation of Text without the Ruling)
- **Bab: الآيات التي حذفت من المصحف وبقي حكمها (Al-Ayat allati huthifat min al-Mushaf wa baqiya hukmuha - Verses removed from recitation but their legal weight remains)**

### 2.3 نسخ الحكم دون التلاوة (Naskh al-Hukm dun al-Tilawah - Abrogation of Ruling without the Text)
- **Bab: الآيات الباقية في المصحف المنسوخة حكماً (Al-Ayat al-Baqiyah fi al-Mushaf al-Mansukhah hukman - Verses recited in the Quran but legally voided by later revelation)**

## 3. طرق معرفة النسخ (Turuq Ma'rifat al-Naskh - Methods of Identifying Abrogation)
The strict epistemological tools scholars use to prove that a verse has indeed been abrogated.

### 3.1 الأدلة النقلية (Al-Adillah al-Naqliyyah - Transmitted Evidences)
- **Bab: النص الصريح من النبي (Al-Nass al-Sarih min al-Nabi - Explicit statements from the Prophet specifying abrogation)**
- **Bab: قول الصحابي (Qawl al-Sahabi - The statement of a Companion clarifying the timeline of revelation)**

### 3.2 التاريخ والزمن (Al-Tarikh wa al-Zaman - History and Timeline)
- **Bab: معرفة المتقدم والمتأخر (Ma'rifat al-Mutaqaddim wa al-Muta'akhkhir - Knowing the chronologically earlier and later texts)**
- **Bab: المكي والمدني في النسخ (Al-Makki wa al-Madani fi al-Naskh - Using Meccan vs. Medinan classifications)**

### 3.3 الإجماع (Al-Ijma' - Scholarly Consensus)
- **Bab: الإجماع على أن النص منسوخ (Al-Ijma' 'ala anna al-nass mansukh - Consensus of early generations)**

### 3.4 ما لا يثبت به النسخ (Ma la yathbut bihi al-Naskh - Invalid Methods)
- **Bab: رد النسخ بالاجتهاد والرأي المجرد (Rad al-Naskh bi al-Ijtihad wa al-Ra'y al-Mujarrad - Rejecting abrogation via pure intellect or reasoning)**

## 4. مجالات النسخ (Majalat al-Naskh - Domains of Abrogation)
Identifying the specific subject matters that accept abrogation versus those that are immutable.

### 4.1 ما يقع فيه النسخ (Ma yaqa' fih al-Naskh - Where Abrogation Occurs)
- **Bab: الأحكام الشرعية الفرعية (Al-Ahkam al-Shar'iyyah al-Far'iyyah - Practical legal rulings, commands, and prohibitions)**

### 4.2 ما لا يقع فيه النسخ (Ma la yaqa' fih al-Naskh - Where Abrogation Does Not Occur)
- **Bab: العقائد وأصول الدين (Al-Aqa'id wa usul al-Din - Creed, theology, and the nature of God)**
- **Bab: الأخبار والقصص (Al-Akhbar wa al-Qisas - Historical narratives and stories of past prophets)**
- **Bab: أصول الأخلاق والفضائل (Usul al-Akhlaq wa al-Fada'il - Universal ethics and foundational morals)**

## 5. أنواع الناسخ (Anwa' al-Nasikh - Types of Abrogating Texts)
The hierarchical relationship between the Quran and the Sunnah regarding abrogation.

### 5.1 نسخ القرآن بالقرآن (Naskh al-Quran bi al-Quran - Quran abrogating Quran)
- **Bab: الأمثلة المتفق عليها (Al-Amthilah al-Muttafaq 'alayha - Unanimously agreed-upon examples, e.g., the waiting period of widows)**

### 5.2 نسخ السنة بالقرآن (Naskh al-Sunnah bi al-Quran - Sunnah abrogating Sunnah)
- **Bab: نسخ حكم ثبت بالسنة بنص قرآني (Naskh hukm thabata bi al-Sunnah bi-nass Qurani - Quran lifting a ruling originally established by Sunnah)**

### 5.3 نسخ القرآن بالسنة (Naskh al-Quran bi al-Sunnah - Sunnah abrogating Quran)
- **Bab: اختلاف الأصوليين في جوازه (Ikhtilaf al-Usuliyyin fi jawazih - Methodological dispute among jurists)**
- **Bab: نسخ القرآن بالسنة المتواترة (Naskh al-Quran bi al-Sunnah al-Mutawatirah - Debates on Mass-transmitted Sunnah)**

### 5.4 نسخ السنة بالسنة (Naskh al-Sunnah bi al-Sunnah - Sunnah abrogating Sunnah)
- **Bab: نسخ الآحاد بالآحاد والمتواتر بالمتواتر (Naskh al-Ahad bi al-Ahad wa al-Mutawatir bi al-Mutawatir - Isolated reports and mass-transmitted reports)**
