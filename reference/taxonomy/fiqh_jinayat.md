# Taxonomy of Jinayat wa Hudud - (تصنيف الجنايات والحدود)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Al-Jinayat wa al-Hudud (الجنايات والحدود), the branch of Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) dealing with criminal law, penal codes, retaliation, and judicial punishments.

## 1. كتاب الجنايات (Kitab al-Jinayat - The Book of Crimes and Retaliation)
The rulings regarding crimes committed against human life and bodily integrity, retribution, and compensation.

### 1.1 أنواع القتل والجراح (Anwa' al-Qatl wa al-Jirah - Types of Homicide and Injury)
- **Bab: القتل العمد (Al-Qatl al-'Amd - Intentional/Premeditated Homicide)**
- **Bab: القتل شبه العمد (Al-Qatl Shibh al-'Amd - Semi-Intentional Homicide)**
- **Bab: القتل الخطأ وما أجرى مجراه (Al-Qatl al-Khata' - Accidental Homicide and Equivalents)**
- **Bab: الجناية على ما دون النفس (Al-Jinayah 'ala ma dun al-Nafs - Crimes against body parts)**

### 1.2 القصاص (Al-Qisas - Retaliation in Kind)
- **Bab: شروط وجوب القصاص في النفس (Shurut Wujub al-Qisas - Conditions for Retaliation in Life)**
- **Bab: شروط استيفاء القصاص (Shurut Istifa' al-Qisas - Conditions for Executing Retaliation)**
- **Bab: القصاص في الأطراف والجروح (Al-Qisas fi al-Atraf wa al-Juruh - Retaliation for Limbs and Wounds)**
- **Bab: العفو عن القصاص والصلح (Al-'Afw 'an al-Qisas wa al-Sulh - Pardoning and Settlement)**

### 1.3 الديات (Al-Diyat - Blood Money and Compensation)
- **Bab: مقادير الديات في النفس (Maqadir al-Diyat fi al-Nafs - Prescribed Amounts for a Life)**
- **Bab: ديات الأعضاء والمنافع (Diyat al-A'da' wa al-Manafi' - Compensation for Limbs and Functions)**
- **Bab: حكم الغرة في الجنين (Hukm al-Ghurrah fi al-Janin - Compensation for Miscarriage)**
- **Bab: العاقلة وتحمل الدية (Al-'Aqilah wa Tahammul al-Diyah - Responsibility of Paternal Relatives)**

## 2. كتاب الحدود (Kitab al-Hudud - The Book of Prescribed Punishments)
Crimes that violate the "Boundaries of Allah," carrying fixed, unalterable punishments.

### 2.1 حد الزنا والقذف (Hadd al-Zina wa al-Qadhf - Fornication and Slander)
- **Bab: شروط ثبوت الزنا (Shurut Thubut al-Zina - Conditions for Establishing Zina)**
- **Bab: حد المحصن وغير المحصن (Hadd al-Muhsan wa Ghayr al-Muhsan - Married vs. Unmarried Offender)**
- **Bab: حد القذف (Hadd al-Qadhf - Punishment for Slander)**

### 2.2 حد السرقة والحرابة (Hadd al-Sariqah wa al-Hirabah - Theft and Highway Robbery)
- **Bab: شروط السرقة (Shurut al-Sariqah - Conditions of Punishable Theft)**
- **Bab: النصاب والحرز (Al-Nisab wa al-Hirz - Value Threshold and Safekeeping)**
- **Bab: حد قطاع الطريق أو الحرابة (Hadd Qutta' al-Tariq / Al-Hirabah - Highway Robbery/Terror)**

### 2.3 حد المسكرات (Hadd al-Muskirat - Punishment for Intoxicants)
- **Bab: ما يوجب حد الخمر (Ma Yujib Hadd al-Khamr - Requirement for Intoxicant Punishment)**
- **Bab: مقدار حد الشرب (Miqdar Hadd al-Shurb - Number of Lashes)**

### 2.4 الردة والبغي (Al-Riddah wa al-Baghy - Apostasy and Rebellion)
- **Bab: أحكام المرتد والاستتابة (Ahkam al-Murtadd wa al-Istitabah - Apostasy and Repentance)**
- **Bab: أحكام قتال البغاة (Ahkam Qital al-Bughah - Fighting Armed Rebels)**

## 3. كتاب التعزير (Kitab al-Ta'zir - The Book of Discretionary Punishments)
Jurisprudence for crimes without a fixed prescribed punishment (Hadd) left to the judge's discretion.

### 3.1 أحكام التعزير (Ahkam al-Ta'zir - Rulings of Discretionary Punishment)
- **Bab: المخالفات والمعاصي التي فيها تعزير (Al-Mukhalafat wa al-Ma'asi - Sins Subject to Ta'zir)**
- **Bab: أنواع العقوبات التعزيرية (Anwa' al-'Uqubat al-Ta'ziriyyah - Types: Imprisonment, Fines, Exiling)**
- **Bab: ضوابط التعزير وحدوده (Dawabit al-Ta'zir wa Hududuh - Limits on Judicial Discretion)**

## 4. الإثبات الجنائي (Al-Ithbat al-Jina'i - Criminal Evidence)
Stringent procedural rules required to convict a person under Islamic law.

### 4.1 طرق الإثبات في الجرائم (Turuq al-Ithbat fi al-Jara'im - Methods of Proof)
- **Bab: الإقرار والرجوع فيه (Al-Iqrar wa al-Ruju' fih - Confession and Retraction)**
- **Bab: نصاب الشهادة في الحدود والجنايات (Nisab al-Shahadah - Number/Criteria of Witnesses)**
- **Bab: درء الحدود بالشبهات (Dar' al-Hudud bi-al-Shubuhat - Averting Punishment due to Doubt)**
