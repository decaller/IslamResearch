# Taxonomy of Tafsir (Quranic Exegesis) - (تصنيف علم التفسير)

This document provides a deep, structural breakdown of the discipline of Tafsir (التفسير), categorized by its theoretical principles, methodological branches, and specific academic topics (Abwaab - أبواب).

## 1. أصول التفسير ومقدماته (Usul al-Tafsir & Muqaddimat - Principles and Prolegomena)
The foundational rules, methodologies, and prerequisites required before engaging in the exegesis of the Quran.

### 1.1 المُفَسِّر (Al-Mufassir - The Exegete)
- **Bab: شروط المفسر (Shurut al-Mufassir - Required Conditions & Qualifications of the Exegete)**
- **Bab: آداب المفسر (Adab al-Mufassir - Etiquette and Ethics of the Exegete)**
- **Bab: مراتب المفسرين (Maratib al-Mufassirin - Ranks/Levels of the Exegetes)**

### 1.2 قواعد التفسير (Qawa'id al-Tafsir - Rules of Interpretation)
- **Bab: المحكم والمتشابه (Al-Muhkam wa al-Mutashabih - Clear vs. Ambiguous Verses)**
- **Bab: العام والخاص (Al-Aam wa al-Khas - General vs. Specific Meanings)**
- **Bab: المطلق والمقيد (Al-Mutlaq wa al-Muqayyad - Absolute vs. Restricted Rulings)**
- **Bab: الحقيقة والمجاز (Al-Haqiqah wa al-Majaz - Literal vs. Metaphorical Usage)**
- **Bab: المنطوق والمفهوم (Al-Mantuq wa al-Mafhum - Explicit vs. Implied Meanings)**

### 1.3 أسباب النزول والنسخ (Asbab al-Nuzul wa al-Naskh - Context and Abrogation)
- **Bab: معرفة أسباب النزول وأهميتها (Ma'rifat Asbab al-Nuzul wa Ahammiyatuha - Knowing Contexts of Revelation and its Importance)**
- **Bab: تعدد الأسباب والنازل واحد (Ta'addud al-Asbab wa al-Nazil Wahid - Multiple Contexts for a Single Revelation)**
- **Bab: معرفة الناسخ والمنسوخ في التفسير (Ma'rifat al-Nasikh wa al-Mansukh fi al-Tafsir - Identifying Abrogating and Abrogated in Exegesis)**

## 2. التفسير بالمأثور (Tafsir bi al-Ma'thur - Exegesis by Narration/Tradition)
Interpretation of the Quran relying strictly on transmitted texts and historical chains.

### 2.1 مصادر المأثور (Masadir al-Ma'thur - Sources of Transmitted Exegesis)
- **Bab: تفسير القرآن بالقرآن (Tafsir al-Quran bi al-Quran - Interpreting the Quran with other Quranic verses)**
- **Bab: تفسير القرآن بالسنة (Tafsir al-Quran bi al-Sunnah - Interpreting the Quran through Prophetic Hadith)**
- **Bab: تفسير الصحابة (Tafsir al-Sahabah - Exegesis based on the statements of the Companions)**
- **Bab: تفسير التابعين (Tafsir al-Tabi'in - Exegesis based on the statements of the Successors)**

### 2.2 الإسرائيليات والموضوعات (Al-Isra'iliyyat wa al-Mawdu'at - Judeo-Christian & Fabricated Traditions)
- **Bab: حكم رواية الإسرائيليات (Hukm Riwayat al-Isra'iliyyat - The Ruling on Narrating Judeo-Christian reports)**
- **Bab: أقسام الإسرائيليات (Aqsam al-Isra'iliyyat - Classifications of Judeo-Christian reports: Accepted, Rejected, Silent)**
- **Bab: الدخيل في التفسير (Al-Dakhil fi al-Tafsir - Inserted/Fabricated elements in Exegesis)**

## 3. التفسير بالرأي والدراية (Tafsir bi al-Ra'y / al-Dirayah - Exegesis by Reason/Knowledge)
Interpretation of the Quran based on linguistic mastery, reasoning, and deductive knowledge.

### 3.1 أحكام الرأي (Ahkam al-Ra'y - Rulings on Rational Exegesis)
- **Bab: الرأي المحمود أو الجائز (Al-Ra'y al-Mahmud / al-Ja'iz - Praiseworthy/Permissible Rational Exegesis)**
- **Bab: الرأي المذموم أو الباطل (Al-Ra'y al-Madhmoom / al-Batil - Blameworthy/Invalid Rational Exegesis)**

### 3.2 التفسير اللغوي والبياني (Al-Tafsir al-Lughawi wa al-Bayani - Linguistic and Rhetorical Exegesis)
- **Bab: إعراب القرآن (I'rab al-Quran - Grammatical Parsing and its impact on meaning)**
- **Bab: غريب القرآن (Gharib al-Quran - Explaining rare or difficult vocabulary)**
- **Bab: بلاغة القرآن (Balaghat al-Quran - Analyzing Rhetoric, Metaphors, and Imagery)**
- **Bab: الوجوه والنظائر (Al-Wujuh wa al-Naza'ir - Homonyms and Synonyms in the Quran)**

## 4. التفاسير النوعية (Al-Tafasir al-Naw'iyyah - Specialized / Categorical Exegesis)
Exegesis focused on specific themes, methodologies, or objectives.

### 4.1 التفسير الفقهي/أحكام القرآن (Al-Tafsir al-Fiqhi / Ahkam al-Quran - Jurisprudential Exegesis)
- **Bab: استنباط الأحكام الشرعية (Istinbat al-Ahkam al-Shar'iyyah - Methodology of deriving legal rulings from verses)**
- **Bab: اختلاف المذاهب في تفسير آيات الأحكام (Ikhtilaf al-Madhahib fi Tafsir Ayat al-Ahkam - Sectarian/School differences in interpreting legal verses)**

### 4.2 التفسير الموضوعي (Al-Tafsir al-Mawdu'i - Thematic Exegesis)
- **Bab: دراسة مصطلح قرآني (Dirasat Mustalah Qurani - Studying the evolution and usage of a specific Quranic term)**
- **Bab: دراسة موضوع في القرآن (Dirasat Mawdu' fi al-Quran - Studying a specific theme, e.g., "Patience," across the entire Quran)**
- **Bab: الوحدة الموضوعية للسورة (Al-Wahdah al-Mawdu'iyyah li-Surah - The thematic unity and central objective of a single Surah)**

### 4.3 التفسير الإشاري (Al-Tafsir al-Ishari / al-Sufi - Esoteric / Spiritual Exegesis)
- **Bab: الظاهر والباطن (Al-Zahir wa al-Batin - The Apparent and the Hidden meanings)**
- **Bab: شروط قبول التفسير الإشاري (Shurut Qabul al-Tafsir al-Ishari - Conditions for accepting esoteric interpretations without violating the literal text)**

### 4.4 التفسير العلمي (Al-Tafsir al-Ilmi - Scientific Exegesis)
- **Bab: الإعجاز العلمي في القرآن (Al-I'jaz al-Ilmi fi al-Quran - Scientific Miracles in the Quran)**
- **Bab: ضوابط التفسير العلمي (Dawabit al-Tafsir al-Ilmi - Guidelines and boundaries for interpreting verses through modern science)**
