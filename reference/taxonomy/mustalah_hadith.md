# Taxonomy of Mustalah al-Hadith - (تصنيف مصطلح الحديث)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Mustalah al-Hadith (مصطلح الحديث). It categorizes the terminology and methodologies used by scholars to evaluate the chains of transmission (Isnad), assess the reliability of narrators, and classify the final status of a Prophetic tradition.

## 1. مقدمات وتعريفات (Muqaddimat wa Ta'rifat - Introductions and Definitions)
The foundational definitions and the basic components of a narrated tradition.

### 1.1 المبادئ الأساسية (Al-Mabadi' al-Asasiyyah - Basic Concepts)
- **Bab: تعريف السنة والحديث والخبر والأثر (Ta'rif al-Sunnah, wa al-Hadith, wa al-Khabar, wa al-Athar - Defining Sunnah, Hadith, Report, and Trace)**
- **Bab: السند والمتن (Al-Sanad wa al-Matn - Chain of Transmission and Text)**
- **Bab: أهمية الإسناد في الدين (Ahammiyat al-Isnad fi al-Din - Theological importance of the Isnad)**

## 2. تقسيم الحديث باعتبار طرقه (Taqsim al-Hadith bi-i'tibar Turuqih - Classification by Routes of Transmission)
Categorizing Hadith based on the number of narrators at each generation.

### 2.1 المتواتر (Al-Mutawatir - Mass-Transmitted)
- **Bab: المتواتر اللفظي (Al-Mutawatir al-Lafzi - Mass-transmitted in exact wording)**
- **Bab: المتواتر المعنوي (Al-Mutawatir al-Ma'nawi - Mass-transmitted in conceptual meaning)**

### 2.2 الآحاد (Al-Ahad - Isolated/Limited Transmission)
- **Bab: المشهور والمستفيض (Al-Mashhur / Al-Mustafid - Well-known: 3 or more narrators)**
- **Bab: العزيز (Al-'Aziz - Rare/Strong: Exactly 2 narrators)**
- **Bab: الغريب أو الفرد: المطلق والنسبي (Al-Gharib / Al-Fard - Solitary: Only 1 narrator)**

## 3. تقسيم الآحاد باعتبار القبول والرد (Taqsim al-Ahad bi-i'tibar al-Qabul wa al-Radd - Classification by Acceptance and Rejection)
Final grading of a Hadith for legal and theological evidence.

### 3.1 الحديث المقبول (Al-Hadith al-Maqbul - The Accepted Hadith)
- **Bab: الصحيح لذاته (Al-Sahih li-Dhatihi - Authentic in and of itself)**
- **Bab: الحسن لذاته (Al-Hasan li-Dhatihi - Good in and of itself)**
- **Bab: الصحيح لغيره (Al-Sahih li-Ghayrihi - Authentic due to corroboration)**
- **Bab: الحسن لغيره (Al-Hasan li-Ghayrihi - Good due to corroboration)**

### 3.2 الحديث المردود / الضعيف (Al-Hadith al-Mardud / Al-Da'if - The Rejected Hadith / Weak)
- **Bab: تعريف الضعيف وحكم العمل به (Ta'rif al-Da'if wa Hukm al-'Amal bih - Definition of Weak and ruling on acting upon it)**

## 4. أسباب رد الحديث (Asbab Radd al-Hadith - Causes of Rejection)
Flaws in chain continuity or narrator integrity.

### 4.1 الرد بسبب سقط في الإسناد (Al-Radd bi-sabab saqt fi al-Isnad - Missing Link in Chain)
- **Bab: المعلق (Al-Mu'allaq - Suspended)**
- **Bab: المرسل (Al-Mursal - Forwarded/Missing Companion)**
- **Bab: المعضل (Al-Mu'dal - Problematic: Missing 2+ consecutive)**
- **Bab: المنقطع (Al-Munqati' - Broken middle)**
- **Bab: المدلس (Al-Mudallas - Concealed link)**
- **Bab: المرسل الخفي (Al-Mursal al-Khafi - Hidden Forwarding)**

### 4.2 الرد بسبب طعن في الراوي (Al-Radd bi-sabab ta'n fi al-Rawi - Criticism of Narrator)
- **Bab: الموضوع (Al-Mawdu' - Fabricated/Forged)**
- **Bab: المتروك (Al-Matruk - Discarded/Accused of lying)**
- **Bab: المنكر (Al-Munkar - Denounced)**
- **Bab: الشاذ (Al-Shadh - Anomalous)**
- **Bab: المعلل (Al-Mu'allal - Defective/Hidden flaw)**
- **Bab: المدرج (Al-Mudraj - Interpolated)**
- **Bab: المقلوب (Al-Maqlub - Reversed names/text)**
- **Bab: المضطرب (Al-Mudtarib - Shaky/Reconcilable conflict)**

## 5. تقسيم الحديث باعتبار من أضيف إليه (Taqsim al-Hadith bi-i'tibar man udifa ilayh - Classification by Attribution)
Categorizing based on the source of the statement or action.

### 5.1 مراتب الإضافة (Maratib al-Idafah - Levels of Attribution)
- **Bab: الحديث القدسي (Al-Hadith al-Qudsi - Divine Report)**
- **Bab: الحديث المرفوع (Al-Hadith al-Marfu' - Attributed to the Prophet PBUH)**
- **Bab: الحديث الموقوف (Al-Hadith al-Mawquf - Attributed to a Companion)**
- **Bab: الحديث المقطوع (Al-Hadith al-Maqtu' - Attributed to a Successor)**

## 6. تحمل الحديث وأداؤه (Tahammul al-Hadith wa Ada'uhu - Receiving and Transmitting)
Pedagogical rules for official reception and transmission of Hadith.

### 6.1 طرق التحمل (Turuq al-Tahammul - Methods of Receiving)
- **Bab: السماع من لفظ الشيخ (Al-Sama' min lafz al-Shaykh - Direct hearing)**
- **Bab: القراءة على الشيخ / العرض (Al-Qira'ah 'ala al-Shaykh - Reading back)**
- **Bab: الإجازة (Al-Ijazah - Official permission)**
- **Bab: المناولة والمكاتبة (Al-Munawalah wa al-Makatabah - Handover/Written)**
- **Bab: الوجادة (Al-Wijdah - Finding a scholar's book)**

### 6.2 صيغ الأداء (Siyagh al-Ada' - Phrasing of Transmission)
- **Bab: مراتب الألفاظ: حدثنا، أخبرنا، عن (Maratib al-Alfadh - Hierarchy of phrases)**
- **Bab: المعنعن والمؤنن (Al-Mu'an'an wa al-Mu'annan - "From" and "That" reports)**
