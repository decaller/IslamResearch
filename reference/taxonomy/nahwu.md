# Taxonomy of Nahwu - (تصنيف النحو)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Ilm al-Nahwu (علم النحو), the discipline governing Arabic sentence structure, grammatical states (I'rab), and the rules dictating the endings of words based on their function within a sentence.

## 1. مقدمات وأقسام الكلمة (Muqaddimat wa Aqsam al-Kalimah - Introductions)
Foundational concepts of speech and word divisions.

### 1.1 الكلام وما يتألف منه (Al-Kalam wa ma Yata'allafu minh - Compositions of Speech)
- **Bab: تعريف الكلام (Definition of meaningful Speech)**
- **Bab: الاسم وعلاماته (The Noun and its Identifiers)**
- **Bab: الفعل وعلاماته (The Verb and its Identifiers)**
- **Bab: الحرف (The Particle/Preposition)**

### 1.2 الإعراب والبناء (Al-I'rab wa al-Bina' - Inflection and Fixed Endings)
- **Bab: أقسام الإعراب (The Four Grammatical States: Raf', Nasb, Khafd/Jarr, Jazm)**
- **Bab: العلامات الأصلية والفرعية (Primary and Secondary Signs of Inflection)**
- **Bab: المعربات بالحركات والحروف (Words declined by Vowels vs. Letters)**

## 2. المرفوعات من الأسماء (Al-Marfu'at min al-Asma' - Nominative Nouns)
Nouns in a state of 'Raf' (Subjects and core components).

### 2.1 عمدة الجملة ('Umdat al-Jumlah - The Core of the Sentence)
- **Bab: المبتدأ والخبر (The Subject and the Predicate of a Nominal Sentence)**
- **Bab: الفاعل (The Doer/Agent in a Verbal Sentence)**
- **Bab: نائب الفاعل (The Deputy/Passive Subject)**

### 2.2 النواسخ (Al-Nawasikh - Modifiers/Abrogators)
- **Bab: كان وأخواتها (Kana and its Sisters: Nominative Subject, Accusative Predicate)**
- **Bab: إن وأخواتها (Inna and its Sisters: Accusative Subject, Nominative Predicate)**
- **Bab: ظن وأخواتها (Zanna and its Sisters: Double Accusative Objects)**

## 3. المنصوبات من الأسماء (Al-Mansubat min al-Asma' - Accusative Nouns)
Nouns in a state of 'Nasb' (Objects and situational modifiers).

### 3.1 المفاعيل (Al-Mafa'il - The Five Objects)
- **Bab: المفعول به (The Direct Object)**
- **Bab: المفعول المطلق (The Absolute Object / Cognate Accusative)**
- **Bab: المفعول فيه أو الظرف (The Object of Time and Place / Adverb)**
- **Bab: المفعول لأجله (The Object of Reason/Purpose)**
- **Bab: المفعول معه (The Object of Accompaniment)**

### 3.2 منصوبات أخرى (Mansubat Ukhra - Other Accusative Nouns)
- **Bab: الحال (The Circumstantial State/Condition)**
- **Bab: التمييز (The Specification/Clarification)**
- **Bab: الاستثناء (The Exception)**
- **Bab: المنادى (The Vocative / Addressed Person)**
- **Bab: اسم لا النافية للجنس (The Noun of the Absolute Negating 'La')**

## 4. المجرورات من الأسماء (Al-Majrurat min al-Asma' - Genitive Nouns)
Nouns in a state of 'Jarr' (Prepositions and possession).

### 4.1 أسباب الجر (Asbab al-Jarr - Causes of the Genitive State)
- **Bab: المجرور بحرف الجر (Genitive by Preposition)**
- **Bab: المجرور بالإضافة (Genitive by Annexation/Possession)**
- **Bab: المجرور بالتبعية (Genitive by being an Appositive)**

## 5. التوابع (Al-Tawabi' - Appositives / Followers)
Words that copy the grammatical state of their attached word.

### 5.1 أقسام التوابع (Aqsam al-Tawabi' - Categories of Appositives)
- **Bab: النعت أو الصفة (The Adjective)**
- **Bab: العطف (The Conjunction)**
- **Bab: التوكيد (The Corroboration / Emphasis)**
- **Bab: البدل (The Substitution / Apposition)**

## 6. الأفعال وأحكامها (Al-Af'al wa Ahkamuha - Verbs and their Rulings)
Grammatical states specific to verbs (primarily the Present Tense).

### 6.1 أحكام الفعل المضارع (Ahkam al-Fi'l al-Mudari' - Rulings of the Present Verb)
- **Bab: رفع الفعل المضارع (The Nominative State of the Present Verb)**
- **Bab: نواصب المضارع (Subjunctive Accusative Particles)**
- **Bab: جوازم المضارع (Jussive Truncating Particles)**

### 6.2 الأدوات الشرطية (Al-Adawat al-Shartiyyah - Conditional Particles)
- **Bab: أدوات الشرط الجازمة (Conditional Particles making two verbs Jussive)**
