# Taxonomy of Usul al-Fiqh - (تصنيف أصول الفقه)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Usul al-Fiqh (أصول الفقه), the methodological science of Islamic Jurisprudence. It outlines the nature of legal rulings, the recognized sources of law, the linguistic rules for text interpretation, and the process of independent legal reasoning (Ijtihad).

## 1. الأحكام الشرعية (Al-Ahkam al-Shar'iyyah - The Islamic Rulings)
The foundational definitions of what constitutes a ruling, who issues it, and its classifications.

### 1.1 الحكم التكليفي (Al-Hukm al-Taklifi - Prescriptive Rulings)
- **Bab: الواجب أو الفرض (Al-Wajib / Al-Fard - The Obligatory)**
- **Bab: المندوب أو السنة (Al-Mandub / Al-Sunnah - The Recommended)**
- **Bab: المحرم (Al-Muharram - The Prohibited)**
- **Bab: المكروه (Al-Makruh - The Disliked)**
- **Bab: المباح (Al-Mubah - The Permissible)**

### 1.2 الحكم الوضعي (Al-Hukm al-Wad'i - Declaratory Rulings)
- **Bab: السبب والشرط والمانع (Al-Sabab, al-Shart, wa al-Mani' - Cause, Condition, Impediment)**
- **Bab: الصحيح والفاسد والباطل (Al-Sahih, al-Fasid, wa al-Batil - Valid, Defective, and Void Acts)**
- **Bab: الرخصة والعزيمة (Al-Rukhsa wa al-'Azimah - Concession vs. Strict Initial Ruling)**

### 1.3 أركان الحكم (Arkan al-Hukm - Pillars of the Ruling)
- **Bab: الحاكم (Al-Hakim - The Lawgiver / Allah)**
- **Bab: المحكوم فيه (Al-Mahkum Fih - The Act of the Accountable Subject)**
- **Bab: المحكوم عليه وأهلية المكلف (Al-Mahkum 'Alayh - The Accountable Subject and Capacity)**

## 2. الأدلة الشرعية (Al-Adillah al-Shar'iyyah - Islamic Evidences/Sources)
The recognized sources from which all legal rulings are extracted.

### 2.1 الأدلة المتفق عليها (Al-Adillah al-Muttafaq 'Alayha - Agreed-Upon Sources)
- **Bab: القرآن الكريم أو الكتاب (Al-Quran al-Karim / Al-Kitab - The Noble Quran)**
- **Bab: السنة النبوية (Al-Sunnah al-Nabawiyyah - The Prophetic Sunnah)**
- **Bab: الإجماع (Al-Ijma' - Scholarly Consensus)**
- **Bab: القياس وأركانه (Al-Qiyas - Analogical Deduction and its Pillars)**

### 2.2 الأدلة المختلف فيها (Al-Adillah al-Mukhtalaf Fiha - Disputed/Secondary Sources)
- **Bab: الاستحسان (Al-Istihsan - Juristic Preference / Equity)**
- **Bab: المصلحة المرسلة (Al-Maslahah al-Mursalah - Unrestricted Public Interest)**
- **Bab: سد الذرائع (Sadd al-Dhara'i' - Blocking the Means to Evil)**
- **Bab: العرف والعادة (Al-'Urf wa al-'Adah - Custom and Tradition)**
- **Bab: عمل أهل المدينة (Amal Ahl al-Madinah - The Practice of the People of Medina)**
- **Bab: قول الصحابي (Qawl al-Sahabi - The Verdict of a Companion)**
- **Bab: الاستصحاب (Al-Istishab - Presumption of Continuity)**

## 3. القواعد اللغوية والدلالات (Al-Qawa'id al-Lughawiyyah wa al-Dalalat - Linguistic Rules)
The Arabic linguistic frameworks used to interpret texts and extract intended meanings.

### 3.1 الأمر والنهي (Al-Amr wa al-Nahy - Commands and Prohibitions)
- **Bab: صيغ الأمر ودلالاتها (Siyagh al-Amr - Forms of Command and Implications)**
- **Bab: صيغ النهي ودلالاتها (Siyagh al-Nahy - Forms of Prohibition and Implications)**

### 3.2 العام والخاص (Al-'Aam wa al-Khass - The General and the Specific)
- **Bab: ألفاظ العموم (Alfaz al-'Umum - The Vocabulary of Generality)**
- **Bab: المخصصات المتصلة والمنفصلة (Al-Mukhassisat - Connected and Disconnected Specifiers)**

### 3.3 المطلق والمقيد (Al-Mutlaq wa al-Muqayyad - The Absolute and the Restricted)
- **Bab: حمل المطلق على المقيد (Haml al-Mutlaq 'ala al-Muqayyad - Mapping Absolute to Restricted)**

### 3.4 المنطوق والمفهوم (Al-Mantuq wa al-Mafhum - Explicit and Implied)
- **Bab: مفهوم الموافقة (Mafhum al-Muwafaqah - Implication of Equivalence)**
- **Bab: مفهوم المخالفة (Mafhum al-Mukhalafah - Implication of Divergence)**

### 3.5 الظاهر والمؤول (Al-Zahir wa al-Mu'awwal - Apparent and Reinterpreted)
- **Bab: شروط التأويل المقبول (Shurut al-Ta'wil al-Maqbul - Conditions for Acceptable Reinterpretation)**

## 4. التعارض والترجيح (Al-Ta'arudh wa al-Tarjih - Conflict and Preference)
The methodology used when textual evidences appear to contradict one another.

### 4.1 حقيقة التعارض (Haqiqat al-Ta'arudh - Nature of Conflict)
- **Bab: شروط التعارض بين الأدلة (Shurut al-Ta'arudh - Conditions for Conflict)**

### 4.2 طرق دفع التعارض (Turuq Daf' al-Ta'arudh - Resolving Conflict)
- **Bab: الجمع والتوفيق (Al-Jam' wa al-Tawfiq - Reconciliation)**
- **Bab: النسخ (Al-Naskh - Ascertaining Abrogation)**
- **Bab: الترجيح (Al-Tarjih - Giving Preference to Stronger Evidence)**

## 5. الاجتهاد والتقليد والفتوى (Ijtihad, Taqlid, wa al-Fatwa - Reasoning and Verdicts)
Rules governing legal reasoning and the ethics of religious edicts.

### 5.1 الاجتهاد (Al-Ijtihad - Independent Legal Reasoning)
- **Bab: شروط المجتهد (Shurut al-Mujtahid - Prerequisite Qualifications)**
- **Bab: مراتب المجتهدين (Maratib al-Mujtahidin - Ranks of the Mujtahids)**
- **Bab: تجزئ الاجتهاد (Tajazzi al-Ijtihad - Divisibility of Ijtihad)**

### 5.2 التقليد (Al-Taqlid - Following Scholarly Authority)
- **Bab: حكم التقليد للعامي (Hukm al-Taqlid - Ruling for the Layman)**
- **Bab: التلفيق بين المذاهب (Al-Talfiq bayna al-Madhahib - Combining Views)**

### 5.3 الفتوى (Al-Fatwa - Issuing Legal Verdicts)
- **Bab: آداب المفتي والمستفتي (Adab al-Mufti wa al-Mustafti - Etiquettes)**
- **Bab: تغير الفتوى بتغير الزمان والمكان (Taghayyur al-Fatwa - Change by Time and Place)**
