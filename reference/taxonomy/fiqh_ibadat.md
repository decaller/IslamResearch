# Taxonomy of Ibadat - (تصنيف العبادات)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Al-Ibadat (العبادات), the foundational branch of Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh). It categorizes the five primary pillars of physical and financial worship as they are traditionally structured in classical legal manuals.

## 1. كتاب الطهارة (Kitab al-Taharah - The Book of Purification)
The rulings pertaining to physical purity, which is the foundational prerequisite for most acts of worship.

### 1.1 المياه والنجاسات (Al-Miyah wa al-Najasat - Waters and Impurities)
- **Bab: أنواع المياه وأحكامها (Anwa' al-Miyah wa Ahkamuha - Types of Water and their Rulings)**
- **Bab: الآنية (Al-Aniyah - Utensils and Containers)**
- **Bab: قضاء الحاجة وآدابها (Qada' al-Hajah wa Adabuha - Etiquettes of Relieving Oneself)**
- **Bab: إزالة النجاسة (Izalat al-Najasah - Methods of Removing Physical Impurities)**

### 1.2 الحدث الأصغر (Al-Hadath al-Asghar - Minor Ritual Impurity)
- **Bab: صفة الوضوء (Sifat al-Wudu' - The Method of Ablution)**
- **Bab: نواقض الوضوء (Nawaqid al-Wudu' - The Nullifiers of Ablution)**
- **Bab: المسح على الخفين والجبائر (Al-Mash 'ala al-Khuffayn wa al-Jaba'ir - Wiping over Footwear and Splints)**

### 1.3 الحدث الأكبر والتيمم (Al-Hadath al-Akbar wa al-Tayammum - Major Impurity and Dry Ablution)
- **Bab: موجبات الغسل وصفته (Mujibat al-Ghusl wa Sifatuh - Causes and Method of the Major Bath)**
- **Bab: التيمم (Al-Tayammum - Dry Ablution with Earth)**

### 1.4 الدماء الطبيعية (Al-Dima' al-Tabi'iyyah - Natural Bloods)
- **Bab: الحيض (Al-Hayd - Menstruation)**
- **Bab: النفاس (Al-Nifas - Postpartum Bleeding)**
- **Bab: الاستحاضة (Al-Istihadah - Irregular Bleeding)**

## 2. كتاب الصلاة (Kitab al-Salah - The Book of Prayer)
The rulings regarding the establishment, conditions, and variations of the Islamic prayer.

### 2.1 مقدمات وشروط الصلاة (Muqaddimat wa Shurut al-Salah - Prerequisites and Conditions)
- **Bab: مواقيت الصلاة (Mawaqit al-Salah - The Prescribed Times of Prayer)**
- **Bab: الأذان والإقامة (Al-Adhan wa al-Iqamah - The Call to Prayer and Commencement)**
- **Bab: شروط الصلاة (Shurut al-Salah - Conditions: e.g., Covering Awrah, Facing Qiblah)**

### 2.2 صفة الصلاة وأحكامها (Sifat al-Salah wa Ahkamuha - Description and Rulings)
- **Bab: أركان الصلاة وواجباتها (Arkan al-Salah wa Wajibatuha - Pillars and Mandatory Acts)**
- **Bab: سنن الصلاة ومكروهاتها (Sunan al-Salah wa Makruhatuha - Recommended and Disliked Acts)**
- **Bab: سجود السهو والتلاوة والشكر (Sujud al-Sahw, Tilawah, wa al-Shukr - Prostrations of Forgetfulness, Recitation, and Gratitude)**
- **Bab: مبطلات الصلاة (Mubtilat al-Salah - The Nullifiers of Prayer)**

### 2.3 صلاة الجماعة والأعذار (Salat al-Jama'ah wa al-A'dhar - Congregational Prayer and Excuses)
- **Bab: صلاة الجماعة والإمامة (Salat al-Jama'ah wa al-Imamah - Congregation and Leadership Rules)**
- **Bab: صلاة المريض (Salat al-Marid - Prayer of the Sick)**
- **Bab: صلاة المسافر (Salat al-Musafir - Prayer of the Traveler: Shortening and Combining)**
- **Bab: صلاة الخوف (Salat al-Khawf - Prayer during Times of Fear/War)**

### 2.4 الصلوات الخاصة (Al-Salawat al-Khassah - Specific and Occasional Prayers)
- **Bab: صلاة الجمعة (Salat al-Jumu'ah - The Friday Prayer)**
- **Bab: صلاة العيدين (Salat al-'Idayn - The Two Eid Prayers)**
- **Bab: صلاة الكسوف والخسوف (Salat al-Kusuf wa al-Khusuf - Solar and Lunar Eclipse Prayers)**
- **Bab: صلاة الاستسقاء (Salat al-Istisqa' - Prayer for Rain)**

### 2.5 كتاب الجنائز (Kitab al-Jana'iz - The Book of Funerals)
- **Bab: أحكام المحتضر وغسل الميت (Ahkam al-Muhtadar wa Ghusl al-Mayyit - Rulings of the Dying and Washing)**
- **Bab: تكفين الميت وصلاة الجنازة (Takfin al-Mayyit wa Salat al-Janazah - Shrouding and Funeral Prayer)**
- **Bab: حمل الجنازة والدفن (Haml al-Janazah wa al-Dafn - Carrying and Burial)**

## 3. كتاب الزكاة (Kitab al-Zakat - The Book of Alms/Charity)
The rulings regarding mandatory wealth distribution, thresholds, and recipients.

### 3.1 الأموال الزكوية (Al-Amwal al-Zakawiyyah - Wealth Subject to Zakat)
- **Bab: زكاة النقدين أو الأثمان (Zakat al-Naqdayn / Al-Athman - Zakat on Gold, Silver, and Currency)**
- **Bab: زكاة بهيمة الأنعام (Zakat Baha'im al-An'am - Zakat on Grazing Livestock)**
- **Bab: زكاة الزروع والثمار (Zakat al-Zuru' wa al-Thimar - Zakat on Agricultural Crops and Fruits)**
- **Bab: زكاة عروض التجارة (Zakat 'Urud al-Tijarah - Zakat on Business/Trade Inventory)**

### 3.2 أحكام الإخراج والمصارف (Ahkam al-Ikhraj wa al-Masarif - Disbursement and Recipients)
- **Bab: أهل الزكاة أو المصارف الثمانية (Ahl al-Zakat / Al-Masarif al-Thamaniyah - The Eight Eligible Categories)**
- **Bab: صدقة الفطر (Sadaqat al-Fitr / Zakat al-Fitr - Fast-Breaking Charity at Ramadan's end)**
- **Bab: صدقة التطوع (Sadaqat al-Tatawwu' - Voluntary Charity)**

## 4. كتاب الصيام (Kitab al-Siyam - The Book of Fasting)
The rulings governing the abstention from food, drink, and intimacy.

### 4.1 أحكام صيام رمضان (Ahkam Siyam Ramadan - Rulings of Fasting Ramadan)
- **Bab: ثبوت دخول الشهر ورؤية الهلال (Thubut Dukhul al-Shahar wa Ru'yat al-Hilal - Moonsighting)**
- **Bab: شروط الصوم (Shurut al-Sawm - Conditions of Obligation and Validity)**
- **Bab: مفسدات الصوم والكفارة (Mufsidat al-Sawm wa al-Kaffarah - Nullifiers and Expiation)**
- **Bab: أعذار الفطر وقضاء رمضان (A'dhar al-Fitr wa Qada' Ramadan - Excuses and Making up Days)**

### 4.2 صيام التطوع والاعتكاف (Al-Siyam al-Tatawwu' wa al-I'tikaf - Voluntary Fasts and Seclusion)
- **Bab: صوم التطوع (Sawm al-Tatawwu' - Recommended Fasts: e.g., Ashura, Arafah)**
- **Bab: الأيام المنهي عن صيامها (Al-Ayyam al-Manhi 'an Siyamiha - Forbidden Days for Fasting)**
- **Bab: الاعتكاف (Al-I'tikaf - Spiritual Retreat in the Mosque)**

## 5. كتاب الحج والعمرة (Kitab al-Hajj wa al-Umrah - The Book of Pilgrimage)
The rulings pertaining to the sacred journey to Mecca and its rituals.

### 5.1 المواقيت والإحرام (Al-Mawaqit wa al-Ihram - Boundaries and Consecration)
- **Bab: المواقيت الزمانية والمكانية (Al-Mawaqit - Temporal and Spatial Boundaries)**
- **Bab: الإحرام وأنساك الحج (Al-Ihram wa Ansaak al-Hajj - Sacred State and Hajj Types: Tamattu', Qiran, Ifrad)**
- **Bab: محظورات الإحرام (Mahzurat al-Ihram - Forbidden Acts during Ihram)**
- **Bab: الفدية والهدي (Al-Fidyah wa al-Hady - Expiations and Sacrificial Animals)**

### 5.2 صفة وأعمال الحج والعمرة (Sifat wa A'mal - Description of the Rituals)
- **Bab: صفة العمرة (Sifat al-Umrah - Method of Umrah)**
- **Bab: أركان وواجبات الحج (Arkan wa Wajibat al-Hajj - Pillars and Mandatory Acts of Hajj)**
- **Bab: الطواف والسعي (Al-Tawaf wa al-Sa'i - Circumambulation and Walk between Safa and Marwah)**
- **Bab: الوقوف بعرفة والمبيت (Al-Wuquf bi-Arafah wa al-Mabit - Arafah and Muzdalifah/Mina Stay)**
- **Bab: رمي الجمرات وأعمال أيام التشريق (Ramy al-Jamarat wa A'mal Ayyam al-Tashriq - Stoning and Tashriq Acts)**
