# Taxonomy of Tajweed & Qira'at - (تصنيف علم التجويد والقراءات)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of the disciplines of Tajweed (التجويد) and Qira'at (القراءات), categorized by foundational principles, phonetic rules, variant transmissions, and orthography.

## 1. مقدمات وأصول التلاوة (Muqaddimat wa Usul al-Tilawah - Introductions and Principles of Recitation)
The foundational concepts, ethics, and parameters of reciting the Quran.

### 1.1 فضل وآداب التلاوة (Fadl wa Adab al-Tilawah - Virtues and Etiquettes of Recitation)
- **Bab: آداب القارئ والمقرئ (Adab al-Qari wa al-Muqri - Etiquette of the Reciter and the Teacher)**
- **Bab: سجود التلاوة (Sujud al-Tilawah - Prostrations of Recitation)**

### 1.2 أركان ومراتب القراءة (Arkan wa Maratib al-Qira'ah - Pillars and Paces of Reading)
- **Bab: أركان القراءة الصحيحة (Arkan al-Qira'ah al-Sahihah - The 3 Pillars of an Authentic Reading)**
- **Bab: مراتب التلاوة: التحقيق، الحدر، التدوير (Maratib al-Tilawah - Paces of Recitation: Tahqiq, Hadr, Tadwir)**
- **Bab: الاستعاذة والبسملة (Al-Isti'adhah wa al-Basmalah - Seeking Refuge and Saying Bismillah)**

## 2. مخارج الحروف وصفاتها (Makharij wa Sifat al-Huroof - Articulation Points and Traits of Letters)
The core phonetics of the Arabic language specific to Quranic recitation.

### 2.1 مخارج الحروف (Makharij al-Huroof - Articulation Points)
- **Bab: مخرج الجوف (Makhraj al-Jawf - The Empty Space of Mouth/Throat)**
- **Bab: مخرج الحلق (Makhraj al-Halq - The Throat)**
- **Bab: مخرج اللسان (Makhraj al-Lisan - The Tongue)**
- **Bab: مخرج الشفتين (Makhraj al-Shafatayn - The Lips)**
- **Bab: مخرج الخيشوم (Makhraj al-Khayshum - The Nasal Cavity)**

### 2.2 صفات الحروف (Sifat al-Huroof - Traits of the Letters)
- **Bab: الصفات المتضادة (Al-Sifat al-Mutadaddah - Traits with Opposites: e.g., Jahr/Hams, Shiddah/Rakhawah)**
- **Bab: الصفات غير المتضادة (Al-Sifat ghayr al-Mutadaddah - Traits without Opposites: e.g., Qalqalah, Safir, Tafashshi)**

### 2.3 التفخيم والترقيق (Al-Tafkhim wa al-Tarqiq - Heaviness and Lightness)
- **Bab: أحكام الراء (Ahkam al-Ra' - Rules of the letter Ra')**
- **Bab: أحكام لام اسم الله (Ahkam Lam Ism Allah - Rules of the Lam in the name of Allah)**
- **Bab: مراتب التفخيم (Maratib al-Tafkhim - Levels of Heaviness)**

## 3. أحكام التجويد (Ahkam al-Tajweed - Rules of Recitation / Assimilation)
The dynamic rules that apply when specific letters interact with one another.

### 3.1 أحكام النون الساكنة والتنوين (Ahkam al-Nun al-Sakinah wa al-Tanwin - Rules of Static Nun and Tanwin)
- **Bab: الإظهار الحلقي (Al-Izhar al-Halqi - Clear Pronunciation)**
- **Bab: الإدغام (Al-Idgham - Assimilation/Merging - with and without Ghunnah)**
- **Bab: الإقلاب (Al-Iqlab / Al-Qalb - Conversion to Mim)**
- **Bab: الإخفاء الحقيقي (Al-Ikhfa' al-Haqiqi - Concealment)**

### 3.2 أحكام الميم الساكنة (Ahkam al-Mim al-Sakinah - Rules of Static Mim)
- **Bab: الإخفاء الشفوي (Al-Ikhfa' al-Shafawi - Labial Concealment)**
- **Bab: الإدغام الشفوي / إدغام المتماثلين الصغير (Al-Idgham al-Shafawi - Labial Assimilation)**
- **Bab: الإظهار الشفوي (Al-Izhar al-Shafawi - Labial Clear Pronunciation)**

### 3.3 أحكام الإدغام العامة (Ahkam al-Idgham al-'Ammah - General Assimilation Rules)
- **Bab: المتماثلان (Al-Mutamathilayn - Two Identical Letters)**
- **Bab: المتجانسان (Al-Mutajanisayn - Two Letters Sharing Articulation but not Traits)**
- **Bab: المتقاربان (Al-Mutaqaribayn - Two Letters Close in Articulation/Traits)**

### 3.4 أحكام المد والقصر (Ahkam al-Madd wa al-Qasr - Rules of Prolongation and Shortening)
- **Bab: المد الطبيعي/الأصلي (Al-Madd al-Tabi'i / al-Asli - Natural Prolongation)**
- **Bab: المد الفرعي بسبب الهمز (Al-Madd al-Far'i bi-sabab al-Hamz - Secondary Prolongation due to Hamzah: Muttasil, Munfasil, Badal)**
- **Bab: المد الفرعي بسبب السكون (Al-Madd al-Far'i bi-sabab al-Sukun - Secondary Prolongation due to Sukun: Lazim, 'Arid, Lin)**

## 4. الوقف والابتداء (Al-Waqf wa al-Ibtida' - Pausing and Starting)
The rules governing where a reciter is permitted to stop for breath and how to resume, preserving semantic meaning.

### 4.1 أقسام الوقف (Aqsam al-Waqf - Types of Pausing)
- **Bab: الوقف الاضطراري (Al-Waqf al-Idtirari - Forced/Emergency Pause)**
- **Bab: الوقف الاختياري (Al-Waqf al-Ikhtiyari - Optional/Chosen Pause)**
- **Bab: الوقف الاختباري (Al-Waqf al-Ikhtibari - Testing/Pedagogical Pause)**

### 4.2 أحكام الوقف الاختياري (Ahkam al-Waqf al-Ikhtiyari - Rulings on Optional Pausing)
- **Bab: الوقف التام (Al-Waqf al-Taam - The Complete Pause)**
- **Bab: الوقف الكافي (Al-Waqf al-Kafi - The Sufficient Pause)**
- **Bab: الوقف الحسن (Al-Waqf al-Hasan - The Good Pause)**
- **Bab: الوقف القبيح (Al-Waqf al-Qabih - The Repulsive/Forbidden Pause)**

### 4.3 السكت والقطع (Al-Sakt wa al-Qat' - Breathless Pauses and Stopping)
- **Bab: سكتات حفص (Saktat Hafs - The Specific Breathless Pauses of Hafs)**

### 4.4 الابتداء (Al-Ibtida' - Starting/Resuming)
- **Bab: الابتداء بهمزة الوصل (Al-Ibtida' bi-Hamzat al-Wasl - Starting with the Connecting Hamzah)**
- **Bab: الابتداء الجائز والممنوع (Al-Ibtida' al-Ja'iz wa al-Mamnu' - Permitted and Forbidden Resumptions)**

## 5. علم القراءات (Ilm al-Qira'at - The Science of Variant Readings)
The study of the authentic, mass-transmitted variations in the pronunciation and wording of the Quranic text.

### 5.1 أصول القراءات (Usul al-Qira'at - Foundations of the Readings)
- **Bab: الأحرف السبعة (Al-Ahruf al-Sab'ah - The Concept of the Seven Ahruf/Dialects)**
- **Bab: نشأة علم القراءات وتاريخه (Nash'at 'Ilm al-Qira'at wa Tarikhuha - History and Compilation of the Readings)**
- **Bab: التواتر والشذوذ (Al-Tawatur wa al-Shudhudh - Mass-Transmission vs. Anomalous Readings)**

### 5.2 القراءات السبع والعشر (Al-Qira'at al-Sab' wa al-Ashr - The Seven and Ten Readings)
- **Bab: القراءات السبع من طريق الشاطبية (Al-Qira'at al-Sab' min Tariq al-Shatibiyyah - The 7 Readings via the Shatibiyyah route)**
- **Bab: القراءات الثلاث المتممة من طريق الدرة (Al-Qira'at al-Thalath al-Mutammimah - The 3 Completing Readings via Al-Durrah)**
- **Bab: القراءات العشر الكبرى من طريق الطيبة (Al-Qira'at al-Ashr al-Kubra min Tariq al-Tayyibah - The 10 Major Readings via Al-Tayyibah)**

### 5.3 فرش الحروف (Farsh al-Huroof - Specific Word Variations)
- **Bab: الكلمات المختلف فيها بين القراء (Al-Kalimat al-Mukhtalaf fiha bayna al-Qurra' - Specific words where reciters differ, organized Surah by Surah)**

## 6. علوم المصحف (Ulum al-Mushaf - Sciences of the Written Text)
Associated disciplines dealing with the written orthography and numbering of the Quran.

### 6.1 الرسم العثماني (Al-Rasm al-Uthmani - Uthmanic Orthography)
- **Bab: الحذف والإثبات (Al-Hadhf wa al-Ithbat - Omitted and Retained Letters)**
- **Bab: المقطوع والموصول (Al-Maqtu' wa al-Mawsul - Words written separated vs. joined)**
- **Bab: تاءات التأنيث (Ta'aat al-Ta'nith - Open vs. Closed 'Ta' of femininity)**

### 6.2 ضبط المصحف (Dabt al-Mushaf - Diacritics and Voweling)
- **Bab: تاريخ نقط الإعراب ونقط الإعجام (Tarikh Nuqat al-I'rab wa Nuqat al-I'jam - History of grammatical voweling and dotting of letters)**
- **Bab: علامات الوقف في المصحف (Alamat al-Waqf - Symbols of Pausing in the Mushaf)**

### 6.3 عد الآي والفواصل ('Add al-Ayi wa al-Fawasil - Verse Counting and Endings)
- **Bab: اختلاف الأمصار في عد الآي (Ikhtilaf al-Amssar fi 'Add al-Ayi - Regional differences in verse counting: Kufan, Basran, Medinan, etc.)**
