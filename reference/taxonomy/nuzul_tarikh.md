# Taxonomy of Asbab al-Nuzul & Tarikh al-Quran - (تصنيف أسباب النزول وتاريخ القرآن)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of the disciplines of Asbab al-Nuzul (Contexts of Revelation) and Tarikh al-Quran (History of the Quran), detailing the timeline of revelation, the methodological rules for contextualizing verses, and the historical documentation of the primary text.

## 1. تاريخ النزول والوحي (Tarikh al-Nuzul wa al-Wahy - History of Revelation)
The study of the phenomenon of divine revelation, its modalities, and the chronological timeline of its descent.

### 1.1 الوحي (Al-Wahy - Divine Revelation)
- **Bab: ماهية الوحي وأنواعه (Mahiyyat al-Wahy wa Anwa'uhu - The Nature and Types of Revelation)**
- **Bab: كيفية نزول الوحي على النبي (Kayfiyyat Nuzul al-Wahy 'ala al-Nabi - Modalities of Descent upon the Prophet)**
- **Bab: مراتب وتنزلات القرآن (Maratib wa Tanazzulat al-Quran - Stages of Descent: Preserved Tablet to the Lowest Heaven to Earth)**

### 1.2 المكي والمدني (Al-Makki wa al-Madani - Meccan and Medinan Revelations)
- **Bab: ضوابط المكي والمدني (Dawabit al-Makki wa al-Madani - Methodological Criteria for Distinguishing Meccan/Medinan)**
- **Bab: خصائص السور المكية والمدنية (Khasa'is al-Suwar al-Makkiyyah wa al-Madaniyyah - Thematic and Linguistic Characteristics)**
- **Bab: الآيات المستثناة (Al-Ayat al-Mustathnah - Meccan verses in Medinan Surahs and vice versa)**
- **Bab: ما نزل بالحضر وما نزل في السفر (Ma Nazala bi al-Hadar wa ma Nazala fi al-Safar - Revealed in Residence vs. During Travel)**

### 1.3 أوائل وأواخر النزول (Awa'il wa Awakhir al-Nuzul - The First and Last Revelations)
- **Bab: أول ما نزل مطلقاً (Awwal ma Nazala Mutlaqan - The Absolute First Revelation)**
- **Bab: أول ما نزل في موضوعات خاصة (Awwal ma Nazala fi Mawdu'at Khassah - Firsts regarding specific topics like Wine or Jihad)**
- **Bab: آخر ما نزل من القرآن (Akhir ma Nazala min al-Quran - The Final Verse Revealed)**

## 2. أسباب النزول (Asbab al-Nuzul - Contexts/Occasions of Revelation)
The science dedicated to identifying the specific historical events, questions, or circumstances that prompted the revelation of particular verses.

### 2.1 أصول وقواعد الأسباب (Usul wa Qawa'id al-Asbab - Principles of Contexts)
- **Bab: تعريف سبب النزول وفوائده (Ta'rif Sabab al-Nuzul wa Fawa'iduh - Definition and Exegetical Benefits of Knowing the Context)**
- **Bab: طرق معرفة السبب (Turuq Ma'rifat al-Sabab - Valid Methods of Identification: Reliance on the Sahaba)**
- **Bab: صيغ سبب النزول الصريحة والمحتملة (Siyagh Sabab al-Nuzul - Phrasing of Reports: Explicit vs. Implicit phrasing by narrators)**

### 2.2 تعدد وترجيح الأسباب (Ta'addud wa Tarjih al-Asbab - Multiplicity and Weighing Reports)
- **Bab: تعدد الأسباب والنازل واحد (Ta'addud al-Asbab wa al-Nazil Wahid - Multiple events triggering a single revelation)**
- **Bab: تعدد النازل والسبب واحد (Ta'addud al-Nazil wa al-Sabab Wahid - Multiple verses revealed for a single event)**
- **Bab: قواعد الترجيح بين الروايات (Qawa'id al-Tarjih bayna al-Riwayat - Rules for weighing conflicting contextual reports)**

### 2.3 العموم والخصوص (Al-'Umum wa al-Khusus - Generality vs. Specificity)
- **Bab: العبرة بعموم اللفظ لا بخصوص السبب (Al-'Ibrah bi 'Umum al-Lafz la bi Khusus al-Sabab - The ruling relies on the general wording, not the specific occasion)**
- **Bab: تخصيص العام بسبب النزول (Takhasis al-Aam bi Sabab al-Nuzul - Instances where the context restricts a general ruling)**

## 3. تاريخ جمع القرآن وتدوينه (Tarikh Jam' al-Quran wa Tadwinihi - History of Compilation and Documentation)
The historical tracking of how the Quran was memorized, transcribed, gathered into a single volume, and standardized.

### 3.1 الجمع في عهد النبي (Al-Jam' fi Ahd al-Nabi - Compilation during the Prophet's Era)
- **Bab: كُتّاب الوحي وأدوات الكتابة (Kuttab al-Wahy wa Adawat al-Kitabah - The Scribes of Revelation and Writing Materials)**
- **Bab: حفظ القرآن في الصدور (Hifz al-Quran fi al-Sudur - The primary reliance on oral memorization)**
- **Bab: العرضة الأخيرة (Al-Ardah al-Akhirah - The Final Review with Angel Jibril)**

### 3.2 جمع أبي بكر الصديق (Jam' Abu Bakr al-Siddiq - The Compilation of Abu Bakr)
- **Bab: دوافع الجمع: معركة اليمامة (Dawafi' al-Jam' - Motivations for Compilation: The Battle of Yamamah)**
- **Bab: منهج زيد بن ثابت في الجمع (Manhaj Zayd bin Thabit fi al-Jam' - The strict dual-verification methodology of Zayd bin Thabit)**
- **Bab: خصائص صحف أبي بكر (Khasa'is Suhuf Abi Bakr - Characteristics of the Abu Bakr Codices)**

### 3.3 نسخ المصاحف في عهد عثمان (Naskh al-Masahif fi Ahd Uthman - Copying the Codices in Uthman's Era)
- **Bab: سبب النسخ: اختلاف القراء (Sabab al-Naskh - The Catalyst: Disputes in recitation among provinces)**
- **Bab: اللجنة العثمانية (Al-Lajnah al-Uthmaniyyah - The Uthmanic Scribing Committee)**
- **Bab: المصاحف العثمانية وتوزيعها في الأمصار (Al-Masahif al-Uthmaniyyah wa Tawzi'uha - The resulting Codices and their distribution to major cities)**

### 3.4 ما بعد المصاحف العثمانية (Ma Ba'd al-Masahif al-Uthmaniyyah - Developments After the Uthmanic Codices)
- **Bab: إعجام وتشكيل المصحف (I'jam wa Tashkil al-Mushaf - The historical addition of dots and vowel marks)**
- **Bab: تحزيب وتجزيء القرآن (Tahzib wa Tajzi' al-Quran - The division of the Quran into Juz, Hizb, and Rub')**
