# Taxonomy of Muamalat - (تصنيف المعاملات)

This document provides a deep structural breakdown of Al-Muamalat (المعاملات), the branch of Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) dealing with civil acts, financial transactions, partnerships, and commerce.

## 1. كتاب البيوع (Kitab al-Buyu' - The Book of Sales and Trade)
The foundational rulings governing the exchange of wealth, goods, and the mechanics of a valid contract.

### 1.1 أركان البيع وشروطه (Arkan wa Shurut al-Bay' - Pillars and Conditions of Sale)
- **Bab: العاقدان (Al-'Aqidan - Contracting Parties)**
- **Bab: المعقود عليه (Al-Ma'qud 'Alayh - Subject Matter and Price)**
- **Bab: صيغة العقد: الإيجاب والقبول (Sighat al-'Aqd - Offer and Acceptance)**

### 1.2 البيوع المنهي عنها (Al-Buyu' al-Manhi 'Anha - Forbidden Sales)
- **Bab: بيع الغرر (Bay' al-Gharar - Excessive Uncertainty/Risk)**
- **Bab: النجش وتلقي الركبان (Al-Najash wa Talaqqi al-Rukban - Deceptive Bidding/Caravan Interception)**
- **Bab: بيع العينة (Bay' al-'Inah - Subterfuge for Interest)**
- **Bab: احتكار السلع (Ihtikar al-Sala' - Monopolization and Hoarding)**

### 1.3 الربا (Al-Riba - Usury and Interest)
- **Bab: ربا الفضل (Riba al-Fadl - Usury of Excess in Barter)**
- **Bab: ربا النسيئة (Riba al-Nasi'ah - Usury of Delay/Credit)**
- **Bab: الأموال الربوية (Al-Amwal al-Ribawiyyah - Usurious Commodities)**

### 1.4 الخيار (Al-Khiyar - Right of Option/Cancellation)
- **Bab: خيار المجلس (Khiyar al-Majlis - Option of the Gathering)**
- **Bab: خيار الشرط (Khiyar al-Shart - Stipulated Option)**
- **Bab: خيار العيب (Khiyar al-'Ayb - Option of Defect)**

## 2. العقود المسماة (Al-'Uqud al-Musammat - Nominate Contracts)
Specific named contracts governing delayed exchanges, services, and currency.

### 2.1 السلم والاستصناع (Al-Salam wa al-Istisna' - Forward Sales and Manufacturing)
- **Bab: شروط بيع السلم (Shurut Bay' al-Salam - Future Delivery Payment Conditions)**
- **Bab: عقد الاستصناع ('Aqd al-Istisna' - Manufacturing Contract)**

### 2.2 الصرف (Al-Sarf - Currency Exchange)
- **Bab: أحكام تبادل العملات (Ahkam Tabadul al-'Umlat - Currency, Gold, and Silver Exchange)**
- **Bab: التقابض في المجلس (Al-Taqabud fi al-Majlis - Requirement of Mutual Possession)**

### 2.3 الإجارة (Al-Ijarah - Leasing and Hiring)
- **Bab: إجارة الأعيان (Ijarat al-A'yan - Leasing Physical Assets)**
- **Bab: إجارة الأشخاص (Ijarat al-Ashkhas - Hiring Labor and Services)**

### 2.4 الجعالة والسبق (Al-Ju'alah wa al-Sabaq - Reward Contracts and Competitions)
- **Bab: أحكام الجعالة (Ahkam al-Ju'alah - Reward-Based Contracts)**
- **Bab: السبق والمناضلة (Al-Sabaq wa al-Munasabat - Permissible Competitions)**

## 3. عقود المشاركات والتوثيقات ('Uqud al-Musharakat wa al-Tawthiqat - Partnerships and Securities)
Contracts based on sharing risk and profit, and methods to secure debts.

### 3.1 الشركات (Al-Shirkat - Partnerships and Companies)
- **Bab: شركة العنان (Shirkat al-'Inan - Capital-Labor Investment Partnership)**
- **Bab: شركة المضاربة أو القراض (Shirkat al-Mudarabah - Silent Partnership)**
- **Bab: شركة الوجوه والأبدان (Shirkat al-Wujuh wa al-Abdan - Credit/Labor Partnerships)**

### 3.2 التوثيقات (Al-Tawthiqat - Securities and Debt Guarantees)
- **Bab: الرهن (Al-Rahn - Collateral/Mortgages)**
- **Bab: الكفالة والضمان (Al-Kafalah wa al-Daman - Guaranty and Surety)**
- **Bab: الحوالة (Al-Hawalah - Transfer of Debt)**

### 3.3 التفليس والحجر (Al-Taflees wa al-Hajr - Bankruptcy and Restriction)
- **Bab: أحكام المفلس (Ahkam al-Muflis - Bankrupt Debtor Rulings)**
- **Bab: الحجر على السفيه (Al-Hajr 'ala al-Safih - Financial Incompetence Restriction)**

## 4. التبرعات والأمانات (Al-Tabarru'at wa al-Amanat - Gratuitous Contracts and Trusts)
Contracts based on charity, goodwill, and safekeeping.

### 4.1 التبرعات (Al-Tabarru'at - Charitable and Voluntary)
- **Bab: الوقف (Al-Waqf - Endowments and Trusts)**
- **Bab: الهبة والعطية (Al-Hibah wa al-'Atiyyah - Gifts and Grants)**
- **Bab: القرض الحسن (Al-Qard al-Hasan - Interest-Free Loans)**

### 4.2 الأمانات (Al-Amanat - Trusts and Safekeeping)
- **Bab: الوديعة (Al-Wadi'ah - Deposits and Asset Safekeeping)**
- **Bab: العارية (Al-'Ariyah - Temporary Use Lending)**
- **Bab: اللقطة (Al-Luqatah - Found Property and Lost Items)**

## 5. أحكام الزراعة والأراضي (Ahkam al-Zira'ah wa al-Aradi - Agriculture and Land)
Jurisprudence related to land ownership, farming, and water rights.

### 5.1 المزارعة والمساقاة (Al-Muzara'ah wa al-Musaqat - Sharecropping and Irrigation)
- **Bab: أحكام المزارعة (Ahkam al-Muzara'ah - Farming Partnerships)**
- **Bab: أحكام المساقاة (Ahkam al-Musaqat - Fruit Tree Tending Partnerships)**

### 5.2 إحيء الموات والإقطاع (Ihya' al-Mawat wa al-Iqta' - Reviving Land and Grants)
- **Bab: شروط تملك الأرض الموات (Shurut Tamalluk al-Ard al-Mawat - Claiming Barren Land)**
- **Bab: أحكام المياه والآبار (Ahkam al-Miyah wa al-Abar - Water Rights and Wells)**
