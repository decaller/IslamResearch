# Taxonomy of Islamic Knowledge

This document serves as a static reference for the categorization of Islamic texts and knowledge. Use these categories as labels or target classes for AI classification pipelines.

## 1. Quranic Sciences (Ulum al-Quran)
Focuses on the study of the Quran, its revelation, and interpretation.

- **Tafsir (Exegesis):** Explanation and interpretation of Quranic verses.
- **Tajweed & Qira'at:** Rules of recitation and the various authentic variant readings.
- **Asbab al-Nuzul:** The historical contexts and reasons for the revelation of specific verses.
- **I'jaz al-Quran:** The miracle and inimitability of the Quran.
- **Nasikh wa al-Mansukh:** The study of abrogating and abrogated verses.

## 2. Hadith Sciences (Ulum al-Hadith)
Focuses on the sayings, actions, and approvals of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).

- **Matn (Text):** The actual content of the Hadith.
- **Isnad (Chain of Narration):** The lineage of transmitters.
- **Mustalah al-Hadith:** Methodology and terminology of Hadith criticism (Sahih, Hassan, Da'if).
- **Takhrij:** The process of locating and citing Hadiths in primary sources.
- **Jarh wa al-Ta'dil:** The science of evaluating the reliability of narrators.

## 3. Jurisprudence (Fiqh & Usul al-Fiqh)
Focuses on Islamic law and the methodology used to derive it.

- **Ibadat:** Acts of worship (Salah, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj).
- **Muamalat:** Transactions, contracts, and financial ethics.
- **Munakahat:** Family law, marriage, and inheritance (Mirath).
- **Jinayat:** Criminal law and justice.
- **Usul al-Fiqh:** Fundamental principles and methodology of legal reasoning.
- **Maqasid al-Shariah:** The higher objectives and goals of Islamic Law.

## 4. Creed & Theology (Aqidah)
Focuses on the pillars of faith and belief systems.

- **Tawhid:** The Oneness of Allah (Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma wa Sifat).
- **Iman:** Belief in Angels, Books, Prophets, the Last Day, and Qadar.
- **Comparative Religion:** Study of other faiths in relation to Islam.
- **Sects & Schools:** Historical and contemporary theological movements (Sunni, Ash'ari, Maturidi, etc.).

## 5. Biography & History (Seerah & Tarikh)
Focuses on the life of the Prophet and the history of the Muslim world.

- **Seerah Nabawiyyah:** Detailed life and biography of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
- **Qisas al-Anbiya:** Stories of the earlier Prophets.
- **Sahaba & Salaf:** Lives of the Companions and early generations.
- **General Islamic History:** The Caliphates, Islamic Empires, and regional histories.

## 6. Spirituality & Ethics (Ihsan / Tasawwuf / Akhlaq)
Focuses on inner purification and character development.

- **Tazkiyah al-Nafs:** Purification of the soul and heart.
- **Akhlaq:** Ethics, manners, and social conduct.
- **Adab:** Refined behavior and etiquette in various life scenarios.
- **Dhikr & Dua:** Remembrance and supplication.

## 7. Language & Linguistics (Lughah)
The tools required to understand primary texts in their original Arabic.

- **Nahwu (Syntax):** Grammar and sentence structure.
- **Sarf (Morphology):** Word formation and conjugation.
- **Balaghah (Eloquence):** Rhetoric and stylistic beauty of the language.

## 8. Contemporary Issues
Modern application of Islamic principles.

- **Islamic Finance:** Modern banking, insurance (Takaful), and investment.
- **Bioethics:** Islamic perspectives on medical and biological advancements.
- **Social Justice:** Human rights, environment, and minority rights.
- **Political Thought:** Governance and international relations in Islam.
