سُورَةُ البَقَرَةِ

ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ
r-y-b Noun

Doubt, suspicion, uncertainty

A disturbing doubt that brings unease to the heart.

 ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ ﴿٢﴾
This is the Book about which there is no doubt, a guidance for those conscious of Allah.
الَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْغَيْبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ ﴿٣﴾

Contemplations on 2:2

Theology Certainty

The negation of doubt (لَا رَيْبَ) is absolute. It is not merely stating that the Book is true, but that it is fundamentally structured in a way that repels objective doubt. The use of 'Rayb' over 'Shakk' implies an active, unsettling doubt, which this Book cures.

account_circle Personal Note • Oct 24, 2023
Linguistic Structure

The pause marks (ۛ ۛ) known as Mu'anaqah indicate that if you pause at one, you cannot pause at the other. "This is the Book, no doubt in it, a guidance..." OR "This is the Book, no doubt, in it is guidance...". Both yield profound complementary meanings.
