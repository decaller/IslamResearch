Angelology bookmark

Study of angelic beings, their hierarchies, roles, and interactions with the physical realm as documented across the corpus.

info Overview

In Islamic theology, angels (Malā'ikah) are celestial beings created from Nūr (light). They are entirely obedient to God and execute various cosmic, earthly, and personal duties.

Unlike jinn or humans, they do not possess free will in the sense of choosing to disobey; their nature is inherently submitted to the divine command.
