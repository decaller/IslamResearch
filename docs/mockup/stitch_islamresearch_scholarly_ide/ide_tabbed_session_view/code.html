Tafsir Ibn Kathir

Al-Fatihah 1:3

The Meaning of Ar-Rahman Ar-Rahim

These are two names derived from Ar-Rahmah (the mercy), but Ar-Rahman has more meanings that pertain to mercy than Ar-Rahim. There is a statement by Ibn Jarir that indicates that there is a consensus on this meaning.

In the Tafsir of Ibn Jarir, it is recorded that `Isa (Jesus) said, "Ar-Rahman is He Who shows mercy in this life and the Hereafter, while Ar-Rahim is He Who shows mercy in the Hereafter."

"The Most Gracious (Ar-Rahman) is the One Whose mercy encompasses all creatures in this world, and the Most Merciful (Ar-Rahim) is the One Whose mercy is specific for the believers in the Hereafter."

Allah said, الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ (The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful).
