Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh)

The human understanding of the divine Islamic law as revealed in the Quran and the Sunnah. It expands and develops Sharia through interpretation (ijtihad) of the Quran and Sunnah by Islamic jurists (Ulema) and is implemented by the rulings (Fatwa) of jurists on questions presented to them.
